abstract: >-
  A training-free, one-pass, agent-based framework that inspects a generated image and revises the prompt to repair a named Vietnamese cultural entity while preserving the requested scene details. Raises contract fulfillment over Culture-TRIP from 55.6 to 69.6 (SDXL) and from 59.1 to 67.3 (FLUX.1-dev) on ViCuP, a benchmark of 50 bilingual prompts.
